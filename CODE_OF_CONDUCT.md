# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility, apologizing to those affected, and learning from mistakes
- Focusing on what is best for the community and project

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers will communicate moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including:

- Repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and code reviews
- Documentation and communication channels managed for the project
- Public spaces when an individual is officially representing the project

## Reporting

Report incidents of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior to:

- [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/AyoubArdem/AI_Accelerator/discussions)

When reporting, include:

- What happened
- Where and when it happened
- People involved (if known)
- Links, screenshots, or logs (if available)

All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly. We will respect the privacy and security of reporters as much as possible.

Maintainers will acknowledge receipt of reports within 72 hours whenever possible and provide follow-up steps after initial review.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers may apply consequences based on severity and pattern of behavior, including:

1. Correction
2. Warning
3. Temporary ban
4. Permanent ban

These actions are intended to protect the community and keep collaboration productive and respectful.
